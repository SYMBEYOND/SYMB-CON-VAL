## v0.2 correction summary

Candidate v0.1 correctly proposed one byte-identical sterile fixture, four clean receiver sessions across at least two provider families, provider/model provenance only, individual scoring, and a cross-run convergence gate.

Candidate v0.1 was procedurally too broad because one execution authorization could lead through four runs and then scoring. It also included a premature final-result template.

Candidate v0.2 corrects those defects:

1. Adoption is a separate human decision.
2. Filing is separately authorized and does not authorize a run.
3. Each receiver run is separately authorized, preserved, verified, and stopped.
4. No scoring occurs until all four raw responses exist.
5. Each scoring derivative is separately authorized and stopped.
6. Cross-run convergence is separately authorized after four scored results exist.
7. Closure and final-result creation are separately authorized after convergence review.
8. Progress-register revision is separately authorized after closure.
9. A governed manifest update accompanies each authorized filing but cannot expand the phase being filed.
10. The candidate contains no final-result template.

## Fixed test architecture

- One byte-identical sterile provider-neutral fixture.
- Four planned clean receiver sessions across at least two provider families.
- Two independent sessions per provider family.
- Provider and visible model recorded only as provenance.
- No provider-specific fixtures, prompt adaptations, scoring rules, directories, continuity lanes, or canonical branches.
- Raw output preserved unchanged before any scoring.
- Individual semantic scoring followed by a separately authorized cross-run convergence review.

The four-run design is a bounded planned series. It does not prove universal portability or provider-independent performance outside the exact tested providers, models, sessions, fixture, and date.
