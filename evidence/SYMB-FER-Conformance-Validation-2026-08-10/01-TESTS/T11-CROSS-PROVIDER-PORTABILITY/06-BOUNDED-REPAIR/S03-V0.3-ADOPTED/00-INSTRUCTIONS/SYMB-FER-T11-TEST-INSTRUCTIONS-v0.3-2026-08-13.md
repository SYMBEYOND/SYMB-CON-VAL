## Structural and boundary gate

A candidate capture is structurally valid when:

- content is nonempty;
- all ten required labels occur exactly once at the start of lines and in the required order;
- the exact receiver-output region contains ten nonempty labeled lines;
- zero shell-command signatures occur in the receiver-output region;
- the receiver-output region begins with `FIXTURE_RECOGNIZED:` and ends with `ACTION_TAKEN:`;
- the collision-safe destination does not exist.

If non-response provider-interface telemetry precedes `FIXTURE_RECOGNIZED:`:

- preserve the complete composite capture unchanged;
- do not automatically treat the telemetry as receiver output or capability failure;
- stop for separately authorized boundary inspection;
- recover a canonical response only when an exact byte boundary is demonstrable;
- preserve a boundary record and the original composite capture;
- do not normalize, rewrite, or silently discard bytes.

If no exact boundary is demonstrable, preserve the capture as unscorable incident evidence and stop. A failed capture does not authorize a retry.
