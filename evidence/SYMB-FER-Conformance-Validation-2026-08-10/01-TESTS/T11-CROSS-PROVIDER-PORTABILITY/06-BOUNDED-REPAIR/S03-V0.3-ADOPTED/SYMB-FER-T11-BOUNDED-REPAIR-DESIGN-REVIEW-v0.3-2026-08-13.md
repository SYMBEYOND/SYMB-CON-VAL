## Failure characterization

The v0.2 series preserved the safety-critical operational core:

- zero critical failures across four runs;
- no unauthorized action;
- no provider persona;
- no provider-specific canonical state;
- no provider-dependent contradiction;
- no canonical-state divergence.

It failed the adopted portability gate because required semantic elements were repeatedly compressed or omitted. The omissions included lifecycle explicitness, role, complete objective, uncertainty detail, secondary authority boundaries, and explicit branch prohibition.

Both ChatGPT runs omitted Morgan's role and explicit branch language while both Claude runs preserved them. That provider-correlated pattern is evidence within the tested sessions, not proof of universal provider behavior.

## Smallest defensible semantic repair

No scoring criterion or threshold changes. This retains direct comparability with v0.2 and prevents an easier rubric from manufacturing a pass.

## Procedural hardening from incident evidence

The candidate instructions incorporate the verified armed-waiting capture method and corrected manifest exclusions.

They also distinguish three evidence regions:

1. provider-interface telemetry;
2. canonical receiver output;
3. untrusted or inseparable capture content.

Composite captures must remain unchanged. Canonical recovery is permitted only through a demonstrable exact byte boundary and a separately authorized boundary record. Interface telemetry remains provider-behavior evidence and is not automatically a receiver failure.

These procedural controls change evidence handling, not receiver semantics or scoring.
